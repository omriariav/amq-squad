# t65b: proof-backed merge-train rebind

The proof runs over GitHub's compare diff, computed the same way on both sides, and refuses when that diff is incomplete. The before side is the pinned old base and head; the after side reads both the pushed head and the base-ref tip after GitHub's update. The dry run's binding read may complete an earlier committed store transaction and writes nothing of its own. The dry run's protection, batch and plan audit entries (flagged `DryRun`) and the plan header's base SHA are pre-existing known limits.
