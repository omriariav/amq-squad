## Owner decision (2026-10-22 07:12, recorded for plan revision 2.4)

Omri: "in 3.0 I don't want these approvals and hashes; we will have a lead to make these
calls based on the rules given to it" and "I'm not sure AMQ is asking the human to approve
this stuff every step". AMQ asks once, at launch, for the roster digest; per-merge approval
is amq-squad's own 2.x carry-over (R30, sections 7.1 and 7.3).

Amendment for 2.4 (owner agreed 07:14):
- Merge to the default branch becomes a rules decision by the lead under a standing grant
  set once per repository (CI green, change inside the task scope, no protected paths, size
  limit, no force push). The batch digest over (PR, head SHA) pairs plus base is still
  computed and written to the audit log; nobody signs it.
- The owner-signed typed gate remains for the four irreversible classes: tag and release,
  external send, credential change, growth beyond the grant.
- Manual mode keeps the per-merge stop for an owner who wants it; auto mode is the default.
- The Go core enforces the grant's limits (repository, base branch, CI green, size cap, no
  force push, no protected-path change); the lead decides inside them. A merge outside the
  grant is refused by the core, not by the lead's judgment, and that refusal is what
  escalates to an owner-signed gate. The model is never the enforcement point.
- Branch protection on the server (CI required, no direct push) stays; it is what makes the
  lead's merge safe to automate.
